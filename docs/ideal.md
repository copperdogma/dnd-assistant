# Ideal

A human GM can keep a physical RPG table immersed and moving, bringing the right map, sound, image, reference, or table cue into play at the right moment without losing the thread of the scene.

Preparing and running a session feels lighter. Players understand what is happening, the GM stays in control, and the tools earn their place by reducing effort during actual play.

_Synthesized 2026-10-24 from [Cam's direction and historical sources](sources.md). The optional autonomous DM horizon is described in [scope](scope.md), not a prerequisite for this outcome._
